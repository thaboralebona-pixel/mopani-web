# Customer complaint procedure

## Channels

- A single support number and WhatsApp contact, named by the owner (OWNER: not yet set).
- Complaints received by a driver or marshal are passed on the same day.

## Response times (recommended, not agreed)

- Acknowledge within 4 hours during operating hours.
- Owner reviews every complaint within 24 hours.
- Written resolution within 3 working days.

## Steps

1. Log the complaint in the incident register (complaints are type "order" or "privacy").
2. Confirm the order number and status from the app, not from memory.
3. Check the facts with the seller and the marshal. Record each account.
4. Offer only what the owner has approved. The team must not offer refunds, credits or payments.
5. Close with the buyer in writing, stating what happened and what was done.

## Things the team must not say

- That money has been paid, held, released or refunded unless the owner confirms it from the payment provider.
- That a product is weighed, verified, certified or guaranteed.
- That a driver or seller is "verified" beyond the owner's roster check.

## Legal review required

Refund and compensation terms, consumer protection obligations under South African law, and any statement about seller responsibility. (LEGAL)
