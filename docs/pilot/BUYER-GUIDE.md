# Buyer guide (draft for the pilot)

**Status:** draft. Wording depends on counsel's review and on payments being enabled. Until then, buyers cannot pay through the app.

## What the app does today

- Shows products, prices per unit, stock and the seller's district.
- Shows the delivery routes and the minimum basket for each route.
- Works out a quoted total on the server when you are signed in. The total is not a charge.

## What the app does not do yet

- It does not take payment. Payments are not available in this pilot.
- It does not show a confirmed total until sign-in is available.
- It does not show driver names or vehicles.

## How delivery pricing works

- Delivery is charged per block of up to 5 kg. A product with no recorded weight counts as 5 kg for each line you buy.
- Product weights are seller averages, not weighed at the rank.
- The delivery fee shown on the route is "for each 5 kg or part". A 6 kg order is two blocks.

## Your order, step by step

1. Choose products and quantities. Check the minimum basket for your route.
2. Get your quoted total (when sign-in is available).
3. Ordering is not yet live in the app. The pilot will start with supervised orders placed by the team. An order counts as placed only when you receive the team's written confirmation.
4. A marshal at the origin rank collects from the seller. You will be told when collection is done.
5. A driver takes the goods to Johannesburg. You will see the delivery status.
6. Confirm receipt with the team. (Confirmation inside the app is planned, not yet built.)

## If something goes wrong

Contact support (number to be confirmed by the owner). See CUSTOMER-COMPLAINT-PROCEDURE.md.
