# Stock discrepancy procedure

Purpose: when the goods collected do not match the order or the recorded stock.

## Application versus manual

- The app records stock levels and blocks orders above stock at the time of quoting. Stock can still change between quote and collection, and the server returns a `stock_changed` error in that case.
- Counting, weighing and checking goods at the rank is manual. The app does not verify physical weight. Recorded weights are seller averages (see BUYER-GUIDE.md).

## Steps

1. **At collection (marshal):** count and note each line against the order. Note any shortfall or damage on the collection record.
2. **Seller present:** the seller confirms the count. If the seller disagrees, both record their count and the marshal takes a photo of the goods and the count sheet.
3. **Do not substitute** products or sizes without the buyer's agreement recorded in writing (WhatsApp is acceptable only if the message is kept).
4. **Short shipment:** the order is not dispatched in full. The buyer is told the shortfall before dispatch, and the seller is told the same day.
5. **Damaged goods:** do not dispatch. Record with photo.
6. **Escalate** to the owner the same day for any discrepancy above one order line, or any repeat by the same seller.
7. **Money:** no refund or payout decision is taken by the team. Owner decides, and any money action follows the approved process only (LEGAL: payment handling is not yet live).

## Records

Discrepancy log: date, order number, seller, product, expected, counted, action, owner decision. Keep for the pilot period and review weekly.

## Legal note

Settlement terms with sellers for shortfalls have not been agreed. Do not promise compensation. (LEGAL)
