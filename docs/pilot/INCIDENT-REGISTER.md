## Severity guide

- **S1**: money moved or claimed incorrectly, or personal data disclosed. Owner informed within 2 hours.
- **S2**: order not delivered or wrong goods. Owner informed the same day.
- **S3**: delay or minor app error. Reviewed weekly.

## Note

Incidents involving personal data may require POPIA reporting. Counsel to advise (LEGAL). Do not contact regulators or customers about a data incident without counsel's advice.
