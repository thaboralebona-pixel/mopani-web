# First pilot design (recommendation)

**Status:** recommendation only. Nothing here is an owner decision except where marked. The taxi association agreement is **not** signed. Payment collection is **not** available. Orders in the pilot are supervised and placed by the team.

## Route

- Origin: one rank, to be chosen from Elim or Tshakhuma (recommend the rank with the most confirmed sellers and an agreed marshal). Owner to choose.
- Destination: Johannesburg (the configured pilot destination).
- Reason: one corridor keeps the test surface small and matches the existing route data.

## Scale (recommendation)

| Item                                 | Recommended start                               | Maximum for first 4 weeks |
| ------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------------- |
| Sellers                              | 3                                               | 5                         |
| Buyers (diaspora, known to the team) | 5                                               | 10                        |
| Drivers                              | 1 primary, 1 standby                            | 2                         |
| Marshals                             | 1 at origin, 1 at Johannesburg drop-off point   | 2 at origin               |
| Deliveries per week                  | 1 scheduled run                                 | 2 runs                    |
| Orders per run                       | 5                                               | 10                        |
| Order value per order                | up to the route's agreed maximum (owner to set) | same                      |

Buyers are invited, not public. Sellers are onboarded one by one and each completes a short product and weight check.

## Daily operating process

1. Morning: team confirms orders placed by the cut-off time, stock on each product, and the seller list for the day.
2. Collection: marshal counts and records each line with the seller (STOCK-DISCREPANCY-PROCEDURE.md).
3. Loading: driver checks load against the collection record.
4. Transit: driver updates status at each stage. Marshal records proof of collection.
5. Drop-off: buyer or team member confirms receipt.
6. Evening: daily report (OPERATING-PROCEDURES.md). Incidents go to the incident register.

## Maximum initial volume

Limit by value and weight, not only orders. Recommend no more than one run of 10 orders in the first two weeks, and no order above the owner's agreed value.

## Support and escalation

- Support: one named person on the team, reachable during operating hours (owner to name).
- Escalation: marshal or driver to team lead the same hour; team lead to owner the same day for any S1 or S2 incident.
- Orders waiting for a marshal over 48 hours: escalate to owner (owner decision, not yet made).

## Success criteria (recommendation)

- 3 supervised runs completed without money claims made to buyers or sellers.
- At least 90% of collected lines match the order, or each discrepancy is logged and resolved.
- No S1 incidents.
- Each buyer confirms receipt or the team records why not.
- Driver and marshal feedback recorded after each run.

## Preconditions (must be true before the first run)

- Security items SEC-01, SEC-02, SEC-03 and SEC-06 resolved on the pilot database, or accepted by the owner in writing.
- Credentials rotated (owner action).
- Written confirmation from counsel for the seller and buyer terms (LEGAL).
- Written taxi association engagement, or an owner decision to run without it, recorded in OWNER-DECISIONS.md (OWNER).
- Driver and marshal rosters complete (OWNER).

## Not included in this pilot

Payment collection, escrow release, withdrawals, seller subscriptions, listing fees, youth verification and driver or marshal balances shown to anyone.
